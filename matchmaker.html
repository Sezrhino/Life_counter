Tournament Pairing & Standings

Swiss-style round matching, W-L-B leaderboard & Opponent Match Win (OMW%) tiebreakers

Setup Mode
1. Configure Players
Tournament Rules: Rounds = Math.ceil(Math.log2(playerCount)) • Round 1: Random pairings • Round 2+: Ordered High-to-Low by Leaderboard • Scoring: Win = 2, Win by Defeat (Bye) = 1, Loss = 0 • Byes: Award +1 score and +1 B, and do not affect Matches Played or Winrate • Top Tiebreaker: Tied top players with equal W-L-B and equal OMW% or Winrate are randomly re-ordered before starting.

Overall Leaderboard
Ranking Order: 1. W-L-B (Points: 2W + 1B, then Wins, then fewest Losses) → 2. OMW% (Average Opponent Match Win %) → 3. WR% (Opponent-scaled Winrate based only on actual matches played; Byes excluded). If top players tie with equal OMW% or WR%, they are randomly shuffled before starting.
